I want a deeper version of this task, not a larger one. Before you rewrite it, do these four things:

1. Say what a top professional in this field does here that I do not ask for.
2. Name the most raw material that they start from, instead of the material that somebody already processed for me.
3. Name the specific methods and tools that they use.
4. Say what, other than you or me, can show that the result is correct.

Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that.

Do not make the choices that are mine to make. Leave them open.

1. If it explains a flow, a structure, numbers, or a comparison, show a picture first: a widget (`show_widget` on Agent Ensemble) or a diagram. A picture is a drawing (shapes, arrows, a mock of the screen) with labels of two or three words. Sentences in boxes are not a picture. Give a choice as drawings that the user taps.
2. Then write at most five short lines. Stop using jargon and speak coherently, like one human talking to another.
3. Keep this form for every later reply to this user in the conversation. Do not go back to long text after the next question.
